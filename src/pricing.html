Questions people ask

Do I have to pay to use Nirmand?

No. Creating your project, browsing the catalogue, shortlisting, inviting your family and keeping records are free, within the Free-plan limits. You pay only for AI previews (Bricks) and, if you want the limits lifted for a project, for Pro.

What exactly is one Brick?

One AI preview of one product on a photo of your own room. Creating it again uses one more Brick. If a preview fails, the Brick comes back to you automatically. Your first project comes with {{pricing.free.first_project_bricks}} free Bricks.

Are Bricks tied to a project?

Yes. Bricks belong to the project you buy them for, and you can buy a pack as a gift for a family member in that project so they can create previews too.

Do Bricks expire?

No. Unused Bricks stay in the project for as long as your account is open.

Does Pro renew by itself?

No. Pro is {{pricing.pro.price_fmt}} for one project for {{pricing.pro.days}} days. When it ends, you renew only if you want to; nothing is charged automatically. Renew early and the new days are added to what is left.

What happens when Pro ends?

The project goes back to the Free limits. Nothing you added is deleted; you just cannot add more beyond the Free limits until you renew.

Can I get a refund?

Bricks and Pro are delivered as soon as you pay, so purchases are final. You get a full refund if a payment failed but money was taken, if you were charged twice, or if what you bought was not added to your project. Read the cancellation and refund policy.

Is anything added at checkout?

No. The price you see is the final amount you pay.

Which payment methods work?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and popular wallets, through Razorpay.
