Getting started

What is Nirmand?

Nirmand is one app where your family, architect and contractor plan, preview and build your home together. You create a project, browse {{catalogue.products}} real products from {{catalogue.brands}} brands, see them on photos of your own rooms with AI previews, decide together, and keep every bill, payment and expense in one place.

When can I download the app?

Nirmand is launching soon on Android and iPhone. Get early access and we will tell you the day it is ready.

Is Nirmand free?

Yes. Creating your project, browsing, shortlisting, inviting your family and keeping records are free, within the Free-plan limits. You pay only for Bricks, which power AI previews, and, if you want the limits removed for a project, for Pro. Your first project comes with {{pricing.free.first_project_bricks}} free Bricks. See pricing.

Who can I add to my project?

Family members, your architect and your contractor. On the Free plan a project can have up to {{pricing.free.family}} family members, and up to {{pricing.pro.family}} with Pro. Contractors and architects are not counted in that limit.

AI previews

How does an AI preview work?

Take or choose a photo of your room, pick a product such as a tile, laminate or paint, and Nirmand shows that product on your own wall, floor or ceiling. It usually takes 20 to 40 seconds and uses 1 Brick.

How accurate are AI previews?

They are a strong guide, not a photograph. Colour, texture, size and lighting can look different in real life, so always see a real sample before you buy. Read our AI content policy.

What is a Final Look?

A Final Look combines several of your approved previews into one image of the finished room. It uses {{t.bricks}} Bricks at {{t.label}}, depending on the size you choose.

What if a preview fails?

The Brick comes back to you automatically. You do not need to ask.

Are my room photos private?

Your photos are visible only to you and the project members you allow. To create a preview, the photo is sent to our AI provider without your name, phone number or email. We never sell your data. See our privacy policy.

Bricks and Pro

What is a Brick?

A Brick is a credit for AI features: 1 Brick makes one AI preview. Packs start at {{pricing.from_fmt}} for {{pricing.packs.0.bricks}} Bricks.

Do Bricks expire?

No. Bricks stay in your project for as long as your account is open.

Can I use my Bricks in another project?

No. Bricks belong to the project you buy them for. You can buy a pack as a gift for a family member in the same project.

What does Pro include?

Pro removes the Free-plan limits for one project for {{pricing.pro.days}} days: unlimited documents, bills, payments, expenses, work issues and Family Voice groups, up to {{pricing.pro.family}} family members, downloads and sharing, and {{pricing.pro.bonus_bricks}} bonus Bricks. While a project of yours has Pro, you can also create more projects. Pro costs {{pricing.pro.price_fmt}} per project.

Does Pro renew automatically?

No. Nothing renews or charges you automatically. If you renew early, the new {{pricing.pro.days}} days are added to what is left.

What happens when Pro ends?

The project goes back to the Free limits. Nothing is deleted; you just cannot add more beyond the Free limits until you renew.

Payments and refunds

How do I pay?

Choose the project, pick a Bricks pack or Pro, sign in with your Google account and pay with Razorpay using UPI, a card, net banking or a wallet. What you buy is added to the project as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Is anything added at checkout?

No. The price you see is the final amount you pay.

I paid, but I can't see my Bricks or Pro.

Banks sometimes confirm a payment a few minutes late, and our system adds your purchase as soon as it is confirmed. If it is not there after 30 minutes, write to {{company.email}} with your mobile number and the Razorpay payment ID.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final once they are added to your project. You get a full refund if a payment failed but money was taken, if you were charged twice, or if what you bought was not added to your project. Read the refund policy.
