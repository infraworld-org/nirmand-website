Without the app

  1. Email {{company.email}} with the subject “Delete my Nirmand account”. Send it from the email address on your account, or mention the mobile number you sign in with.
  2. Confirm it is you. We reply within 24 hours and may ask you to confirm with a one-time password sent to your mobile number. This stops anyone else from deleting your account.
  3. We start the deletion once it is confirmed. It then follows the same steps as a deletion from the app, below.

What happens after you ask

  • You are signed out on every device straight away.
  • Your account is deleted 7 days after you ask. Until then you can sign in again and cancel: Profile, then More, then Delete account, then Cancel deletion.
  • If other people are active members of a project you own, you need to hand that project over or close it first. Write to {{company.email}} and we will help.

Want to remove only some data and keep your account? Delete projects, photos, documents or AI images in the app, or write to {{company.email}} and tell us what to delete.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, mobile number, profile photo and city.
  • Your sign-in: you cannot sign in to this account again.
  • Your personal My Home records: IDs, helplines, warranties, society and utility details, and the expenses and contacts you did not share with a project.
  • Your notifications and your push-notification token.

Projects you own

A project where no one else is an active member is closed and hidden. No one can open it after that, including anyone who only had read-only access, such as a contractor whose work is marked complete. What is inside it, such as the address, documents, photos and AI images, is not deleted.

Before you delete

  • Download anything you want to keep, such as bills, documents or AI images.
  • Unused Bricks and any remaining Pro time end with your account and are not refunded (see our refund policy).
  • If other people use a project you own, hand it over or close it first, as explained above.
  • After the 7 days, deletion cannot be undone. If you sign up again later, you start with a new, empty account.
