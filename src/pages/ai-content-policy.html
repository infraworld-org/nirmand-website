What our AI creates

  • AI previews: one product, such as a tile, laminate or paint, shown on a photo of your room.
  • Final Look images: several products you approved, shown together in one image of your room.

To make them, we send your room photo and the chosen product images to our AI providers, Google (Gemini) and OpenAI. We do not send your name, phone number or email. Read more in our privacy policy.

An AI image is an illustration, not a photo

AI images help you imagine and decide. They are not exact. Colours, textures, patterns, sizes, joints, lighting and how a product fits your room can look different in real life. Before you buy or build:

  • see a real sample at the shop, in daylight;
  • check sizes, quantities and fitting with your contractor or architect;
  • confirm the product code, finish and price with the shop.

How we label AI images

  • In the app, AI images are always shown as an AI preview or a Final Look, never as a real photo of a product.
  • When you download or share an AI image, it carries a visible AI label and, where technically possible, information inside the file that marks it as made by AI.
  • Do not remove, hide or crop out these labels.

Rules for using AI features

Use AI features only for planning your own home or a project you work on. Do not upload or create:

  • photos of people, especially their faces, or of anyone without their permission;
  • photos of places you have no right to photograph;
  • images that show nudity, sexual content, violence, or anything unlawful;
  • images meant to mislead, such as presenting an AI image as a real photo of a finished home, a real product or a real property listing;
  • images that copy someone else's design or work without permission.

We may block a request, remove an image or limit an account that breaks these rules. Bricks used on a request that breaks these rules are not returned.

AI in Nirmand News

Nirmand News, our city news service, may use AI to help produce images, graphics or voice-overs. Anything made or substantially changed by AI is labelled as such. See our Nirmand News policy.
